Version 2026-11-04

1. What we do

You send us receipts and booking emails. We read them, check them against refund and compensation rules, and show you money you may be owed. When you approve a claim, we write a claim letter for you. You or we then send it to the company. We do not guarantee that any company will pay.

2. Our fee

Our fee is a fixed percentage of the money you recover through a claim we found (25% unless your account shows a different rate at signup). If nothing is recovered, you pay nothing. You agree to tell us honestly when money is recovered, within 14 days of receiving it. The fee is due when you confirm the recovery.

If we take payment by card, the card you saved is charged automatically after you confirm the recovery. If we invoice you, payment is due within 14 days of the invoice.

3. Your part

  • Only send receipts that belong to you, or that you have permission to share.
  • Check every claim letter before it is sent. Only approve claims you believe are true.
  • Keep your login safe. You are responsible for activity on your account.

4. What we don't do

We are not a law firm and do not give legal advice. We do not access your email inbox or bank account. We do not send claims without your approval.

5. Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings, after any fees that are due have been paid. We may close accounts that break these terms.

6. Liability

To the extent the law allows, our total liability to you is limited to the fees you have paid us in the 12 months before the claim.

7. Changes

If we change these terms, we will ask you to accept the new version before you approve further claims.
