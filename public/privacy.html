Privacy policy

Version 2026-11-04

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and country. Login is handled by Google Firebase; we never see or store your password.
  • Receipts you send us: the files and emails you upload or forward, and the details we read from them (store, amount, date, order number, items, flight details).
  • Claims: the claims we find, the letters, and their outcomes.
  • Payments: if you add a card, it is stored by our payment provider (Stripe). We never see your card number.
  • Security logs: sign-ins and important actions, with the IP address, to protect your account.

What we do with it

Only what is needed to find and claim money you are owed, charge our fee, and keep the service secure. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • Anthropic (AI that reads receipts and drafts letters). It does not use your data to train its models.
  • Google Firebase (login), Postmark (receiving forwarded email), Stripe (payments, if used), and our hosting provider.

How long we keep it

Original receipt files and email text are deleted after 30 days. The details we read from them are kept while your account is open, because your claims rely on them. Everything is deleted when you delete your account. Security logs are kept for up to 12 months.

How we protect it

Receipt files and email text are encrypted at rest (AES-256). Each customer's data is kept separate, and every request is checked against your login. Admin access is limited and logged.

Your rights

You can download all your data or delete your account at any time in Settings. You can also ask us to correct your data, or complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
